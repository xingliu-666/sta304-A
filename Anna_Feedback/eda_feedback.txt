Linear regression on a binary response

In your assumption check, you explain why the linear regression does not work. You note that the residuals split into two lines, the reference lines are not flat, the Q-Q plot does not follow the line, and the residual distribution has two peaks. You then say this is because the response is only 0 or 1. If you already know the response is binary, you did not need to fit a linear model and check its assumptions. You should have used logistic regression from the start.

This is a serious issue. Your proposal says you will use logistic regression, but your code fits linear models with lm(). The two models, model (using spending_money) and model_log (using log_spending), need to be logistic regressions fitted with glm(). You show that you know how to do this in RQ3, where you fit a logistic regression with paying as the response.
